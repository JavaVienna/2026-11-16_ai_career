# 7 Ways You Can Build an Amazing Dev Career in the AI Era

Talk by **[Bruno Souza](https://github.com/brjavaman)** at the
[Java Vienna](https://www.java.wien) meetup on
**[2026-11-16](https://www.meetup.com/java-vienna/events/316166123/)**
([event page](https://www.java.wien/events/2026-11-16-dev-career-in-the-ai-era-java-static-site-generat/)).

## Abstract

Are you also wondering where you fit in all of this? Every day, you are
bombarded with claims that AI will transform software development. Is this an
unprecedented opportunity or an existential threat?

After more than 30 years in the software industry, Bruno has seen technologies
create opportunities, destroy opportunities, and completely reshape careers.
Yet there are always professionals who seem to thrive regardless of the changes
happening around them. Why do some become more valuable while others struggle?

This talk faces the myths around AI head-on and explores how you can create
opportunities in the age of AI instead of being afraid and scrambling to keep
up — because the future may belong to those who understand something deeper than
technology itself.

## Speaker

**Bruno Souza** — Since 1995, Bruno has been helping Java developers advance
their careers and work on exciting projects. A recognized Java Evangelist and
**Java Champion**, as well as a board member of the **Java Community Process**,
Bruno founded and leads SouJava, the Brazilian Java Users Society. In his book
*Developer Career Masterplan*, Bruno shares insights on career development for
senior developers, topics he further explores in his Code4.life project.
